Platform

What We Built

Instead of presenting academic papers as-is, the platform offered four content formats to support different learning preferences and accessibility needs.

Format 01

Structured Summaries

AI-generated text summaries that condense complex papers into concise, readable findings without losing the core research intent.

Format 02

Infographics

Visual breakdowns of key data and concepts, making research accessible for users who engage better with visual information.

Format 03

Audio Podcasts

Listen-friendly audio versions of research content, designed for users who prefer auditory learning or have accessibility needs.

Format 04

Voice Chat

A mascot-based conversational interface allowing users to ask questions about research content in natural language.

Key Challenge

Problem and Response

The Problem

Design and development were running in sequence, not in parallel

The natural instinct was for developers to wait for finalized designs before building. In a 10-week window with a real client, that linear approach would have left too little time for development to produce anything meaningful.

My Response

Adjusted the pipeline so both streams ran at the same time

Developers began building from early wireframes while the UI/UX team continued refining the visual design system. This created an overlapping workflow where development moved forward early and design improvements were absorbed as they came — rather than being a blocker.

The Outcome

Reduced waiting time and kept both teams moving

Communication between design and development improved significantly. The final platform included all four content formats, a refined article profile page, navigation improvements, and a working voice chat experience — delivered on schedule.

Leadership Reflection

What I Would Take Forward

01

Overlap by Default, Not by Exception

The parallel pipeline shift was reactive. On the next project, I would build overlapping design and development phases into the schedule from the start rather than waiting for the bottleneck to appear.

02

Involve Developers Earlier in Design

Getting developers into early design conversations — before wireframes are finalized — surfaces technical constraints sooner and reduces rework later. Collaboration should start at discovery, not at handoff.

03

Flexible Planning Over Fixed Stages

A large cross-functional team with a real client requires a schedule that bends. The most valuable skill was recognizing when a plan needed to change and making that call before it became a crisis.

Final Outcome

An AI research platform delivered to a real client in 10 weeks — with four content formats and a smarter team pipeline.
