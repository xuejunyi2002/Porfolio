About Me

Digital project manager, creative collaborator, and lifelong learner — this is who I am and why I do what I do.

Introduction

I'm Ella Xue — a digital project manager with a background in design, a passion for the games industry, and a habit of caring deeply about the details.

I studied interactive media and have spent the past few years working at the intersection of production, design, and cross-functional collaboration. Whether I'm running a sprint retrospective, syncing with a client on deliverables, or obsessing over an onboarding flow — I bring the same level of intention to every part of the process.

I believe the best projects are built by people who trust each other, communicate clearly, and never lose sight of the human experience on the other end of the work.

My Why

Why PM? Why Digital Entertainment?

01

Why Project Management?

I've always been the person who wants to understand the whole picture — how the pieces fit, where the blockers are, and how to keep people moving forward together. Project management isn't about control; it's about creating the conditions where creative people can do their best work without friction getting in the way.

I get energy from the moments when a scattered team finds its rhythm, when a roadblock turns into a pivot, when a project that felt impossible finally ships. That's why I chose this path.

02

Why Digital Entertainment?

Games and digital media are where I feel most alive professionally. They combine every discipline I care about — design, technology, storytelling, psychology, and community — into a single product. The stakes are high and the craft is demanding, and I find that combination genuinely thrilling.

The games industry is one of the few places where a PM needs to understand VR interaction design AND sprint velocity AND what makes a tutorial feel fair. I love that complexity. I want to build my career inside it.
