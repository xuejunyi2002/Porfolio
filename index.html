What I Bring

Some of my strengths as a creative PM

Structure that keeps teams moving.

I turn ambitious ideas into clear and achievable production plans. By connecting scope, milestones, priorities, dependencies, and team capacity, I help creative teams stay focused and move forward with confidence.

My approach is structured but adaptable. In game and animation production, priorities can shift as ideas develop, assets move through the pipeline, and feedback reveals new needs. I build systems that make those changes easier to manage without losing sight of the larger goal.

  • Sprint planning, retrospectives, and milestone tracking
  • Scope definition and proactive prioritization
  • Risk, dependency, and workload visibility
  • Clear documentation across the production lifecycle
  • Review, playtest, asset, and delivery coordination

Strong teams are built through trust, clarity, and empathy.

I take a people-centered approach to project management. I pay attention not only to what needs to be delivered, but also to how the team is working, where people may feel blocked, and what support they need to do their best work.

Creative production brings together people with different disciplines, personalities, and working styles. I listen carefully, adapt how I communicate, and create space for people to raise concerns and share ideas. When perspectives differ, I help the team move toward a constructive decision while keeping everyone aligned around the shared goal.

  • Building trust and open communication within the team
  • Connecting artists, designers, developers, clients, and stakeholders
  • Recognizing workload concerns and blockers early
  • Facilitating constructive discussions and resolving misalignment
  • Supporting team morale while maintaining accountability
  • Keeping decisions, responsibilities, and progress transparent

Finding practical paths through creative complexity.

My background in design and digital media helps me understand both the creative intention behind a project and the production realities required to deliver it. I approach challenges with curiosity, flexibility, and a willingness to look beyond the first solution.

Whether the team is responding to feedback, refining a concept, resolving a pipeline constraint, or deciding where to focus limited time, I help turn uncertainty into clear and actionable next steps. My goal is to protect the quality and identity of the work while keeping decisions realistic for the team, schedule, and available resources.

  • Creative problem-solving within production constraints
  • Translating broad ideas into achievable production steps
  • Supporting design, art, technical, and production discussions
  • Synthesizing feedback into focused priorities
  • Visual communication of concepts, workflows, and progress
  • Balancing creative ambition, quality, scope, and feasibility
