What I Bring

Some of my strengths as a PM

Structure that keeps teams moving.

I build systems that make complexity manageable — sprint boards, milestone trackers, documentation frameworks — so teams can focus on doing great work, not chasing information.

I don't just manage tasks. I design the workflow that makes delivery feel inevitable.

  • Sprint planning, retrospectives, and milestone tracking
  • Risk identification and proactive scope management
  • Documentation systems that survive the full project lifecycle
  • Delivery on time, with nothing lost in translation

The bridge every team needs.

I sit at the intersection of every discipline — translating between designers, developers, artists, and clients so everyone stays informed, aligned, and motivated throughout production.

Clear communication isn't a soft skill. It's the difference between a successful delivery and a last-minute crisis.

  • Primary point of contact for clients and stakeholders
  • Cross-functional syncs that are actually useful
  • Conflict resolution and team morale management
  • Transparent progress reporting at every stage

PM with a designer's instincts.

My background in design gives me the ability to see products holistically — I can advocate for user experience, challenge design decisions constructively, and keep the creative vision intact under production pressure.

I understand what great looks like, which makes me a better advocate for the teams building it.

  • Design thinking applied to production problems
  • UX advocacy throughout development cycles
  • Playtesting facilitation and synthesis
  • Visual communication of project status and progress
